# Risk aversion utility curves

These diagrams illustrate utility as a function of wealth, comparing risk neutrality with mild, moderate and high CARA risk aversion (alpha = 1, 2 and 4). Wealth ranges from 9 to 11. The CARA curves are positively rescaled and shifted to share the endpoints (9, 9) and (11, 11).

- [Utility curves (v2 PDF)](<risk aversion v2.pdf>): the version paired with the editable source.
- [Editable LaTeX source](<risk aversion v2.tex>): a standalone PGFPlots figure containing the four panels.
- [Earlier PDF export](<risk aversion.pdf>): the other original PDF, preserved for completeness.

The alpha = 2 panel corresponds to the risk-aversion parameter used in the revised article's main comparison. These are utility-function illustrations; equilibrium outcomes under risk aversion are in Results.
